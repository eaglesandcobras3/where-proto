Rosemary feels different from Grayton

Town character: planned and walkable vs older and looser.
ED

Local Editor Verified Local

Published Oct 8, 2026 · 10 min read

Scenic Highway 30A

South Walton’s coastal towns along one scenic road.

1

Understanding the corridor

Fake guide body. Rosemary is planned and walkable. Grayton is older and looser. Both are on the same road.

2

Beach access reality

Stick to marked regional accesses when you need parking, restrooms, and seasonal lifeguards. Private dry-sand areas may be restricted even when the wet sand remains publicly walkable.

Walton County advisory

Pack out chairs, tents, and towels before evening turtle-protection hours. Unattended gear can be cleared.

3

Getting around without the stress

Park once when you can, use the Timpoochee Trail for short hops, and treat golf carts as a town-by-town decision rather than a corridor-wide default.

4

First-day checklist

  • Pick a home-base town before locking lodging
  • Confirm beach access and parking for that stretch
  • Check today’s beach flag before planning water time
  • Ask locals about carts, bikes, and dinner waits
