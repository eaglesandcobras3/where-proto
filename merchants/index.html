Owner questions

Before you ask

Straightforward answers about listings, verified owners, and how things work.

Is the listing really free?

Yes. Every legitimate 30A business gets a full listing at zero cost. We do not restrict hours, photos, or updates behind a paywall. Revenue comes from clearly labeled sponsored placements and partnerships.

I’m already listed. How do I take control of my page?

Submit the form below or use Add your business with your business email. We confirm identity and issue dashboard access — usually within 24 hours.

I run more than one business. Do I need separate accounts?

No. One login switches between locations. Manage a coffee shop, a taco stand, and a bike rental from the same dashboard.

What counts as an update?

Anything timely: seasonal hours, weather closures, happy hour, new menu items, retail arrivals, or flash events. Updates flow to your listing and the corridor feed.

Can people leave reviews or ratings on my page?

No. We don’t do star ratings or anonymous review wars. Visitors ask factual questions in community threads where you can respond with a verified-owner badge.

Where does the FAQ on my page come from?

You curate it in your dashboard. We suggest prompts vacationers ask often — parking, golf carts, pets, peak hours, and more.

My business isn’t a restaurant or a shop. Does this still fit?

Yes. Charter captains, wedding planners, home services, bike rentals, and photography studios are heavily searched categories on WhereTo30A.
