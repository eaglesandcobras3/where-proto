## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the community authority, crisp architectural restraint, and sunlit coastal elegance of an insider locale. The voice is authentic, curated, and deeply grounded—pairing maritime prestige with local discernment.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Coastal Utility**:
- Authoritative deep ocean palettes anchored by architectural chalk-white cards and sea-spray neutrals.
- Strict three-column utility allowing seamless transition between geographical town filtering, active community discussion streams, and contextual regional widgets.
- High-contrast visual markers: crisp emerald interactive accents, tactile counter badges, verified contributor pills, and warm golden amber alerts that signal real-time local conditions.

## Colors

The color palette centers around the deep maritime authority of the ocean and coastal daylight:

- **Primary (`#0c232e`)**: Deep Ocean Navy. Used for the prominent top hero banner, commanding headers, high-emphasis text, and primary action controls.
- **Secondary (`#009688` / `#00897b`)**: Coastal Emerald Teal. Powers all interactive accents, verified contributor status indicators, active tab underlines, and key interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b`)**: Warm Amber. Deployed for warning cards, local advisories, weather/surf alerts, and urgent insider tips.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`)**: Architectural Slate. Governs metadata, secondary timestamps, count labels, and inactive iconography.
- **Borders & Backgrounds**: The foundational canvas rests on `#f8f9fa` with crisp contrast against clean `#ffffff` surfaces bound by deliberate 1px `#e2e8f0` structural outlines.

## Typography

Typography balances clean architectural structure with swift scannability:

- **Headlines & Display (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers crisp geometric authority across hero banners, card titles, and section markers. Tight tracking lends an editorial magazine feel to town headlines.
- **Body & Paragraphs (`Inter`)**: Provides high-density readability for questions, replies, insider recommendations, and long-form community responses.
- **Labels, Pills & Badges (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: High-contrast, semi-bold to bold weights with slight tracking provide instantaneous recognition across filter chips, verified author labels, and numeric count tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a rigid 3-column architecture on desktop and a high-efficiency single-column stack on mobile:

- **Hero Banner Area**: Full-width deep navy container (`#0c232e`) integrating an inline pill search bar with an integrated town selector dropdown.
- **Desktop Grid (3 Columns, >1024px)**:
  - **Left Rail (260px - 280px)**: Sticky navigation displaying towns and topic taxonomy filters with numerical count pills.
  - **Center Stream (Flexible 600px - 720px)**: The primary chronological or algorithmic community feed consisting of question cards and local answers.
  - **Right Sidebar (300px - 340px)**: Contextual modules, including weather/surf advisories, top local contributors, and trending neighborhood discussions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 2-column layout collapsing the left rail into an off-canvas drawer or horizontal top scroller while maintaining the feed and compact right sidebar.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 1-column responsive layout. The navy banner simplifies into a compact search bar with a horizontal snap-scroll town selector rail immediately below. Sidebars convert into contextual bottom sheets or inline cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy emphasizes boundary precision and surface contrast rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Base**: Flat `#f8f9fa` canvas.
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure `#ffffff` framed by an explicit 1px `#e2e8f0` border. A micro-shadow (`0 1px 3px 0 rgba(12, 35, 46, 0.04)`) provides separation without visual weight.
- **Interactive Hover State**: Cards lift slightly with an enhanced edge contrast (`#cbd5e1`) and a soft ambient shadow (`0 8px 16px -4px rgba(12, 35, 46, 0.08)`).
- **Hero & Search Pill**: High-contrast nesting—translucent navy-tinted or crisp white elements set directly against the `#0c232e` backdrop.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Deep ocean overlay (`rgba(12, 35, 46, 0.6)`) with structured elevation (`0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.1)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary bridges architectural cards with organic interactive elements:

- **Structural Cards & Modules**: Defined by a balanced `0.5rem` (8px) radius, reinforcing the clean lines of coastal architecture.
- **Pills & Status Tags**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) badges, interactive search containers, town selection chips, and count bubbles.
- **Nested Badges**: Counter bubbles inside chips maintain circular or fully pill-shaped proportions.

## Components

### Hero Search & Town Selector
- **Container**: Deep ocean navy (`#0c232e`) banner with integrated padding (`space-xl` desktop, `space-lg` mobile).
- **Integrated Search Pill**: Pristine white pill (`9999px`) enclosing an inline town selector trigger, input divider (1px `#e2e8f0`), text input, and an emerald search button (`#00897b`).

### Feed Cards
- **Container**: `#ffffff` background, 1px `#e2e8f0` border, `0.5rem` radius, padded with `space-lg`.
- **Header**: Flex layout with user avatar, author name, and a verified contributor pill.
- **Content Area**: Question/discussion title in `headline-sm` with ocean navy text, followed by `body-base` copy.
- **Card Footer**: Pill badges indicating the town and topic, counter metrics (upvotes, replies, views), and bookmark trigger.

### Badges, Pills & Counters
- **Town & Topic Pills**: Pill shape (`9999px`), `#ffffff` background with 1px `#e2e8f0` border, `label-md`. When active, fills with Deep Ocean Navy (`#0c232e`) and white text.
- **Counter Badges**: Nested rounded micro-pills with `#f1f5f9` background and `#475569` text (or teal tint `#e0f2f1` with `#00897b` text for unread/active items).
- **Verified Contributor Pill**: Emerald teal tint (`#e0f2f1`) background, `#00796b` text, featuring a checkmark icon and `label-xs` typography.

### Alert & Warning Cards
- **Container**: Warm amber card with light amber background (`#fffbeb`), 1px amber border (`#fde68a`), and a 4px left highlight bar in `#f59e0b`.
- **Text & Badges**: Amber-900 headline (`#78350f`) with amber-800 copy, accompanied by an amber advisory pill.

### Sidebar Widgets
- **Container**: White surface with 1px `#e2e8f0` border and `0.5rem` radius.
- **Town Quick Filter**: Vertical stack of towns with hover states transitioning to `#f8fafc`, accompanied by aligned count pills.
- **Local Weather & Tides**: Compact widget showing conditions, wind, and water temperature with emerald accents.

### Form Inputs & Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Deep Ocean Navy (`#0c232e`) fill, white text, `label-md`, hovering to `#00897b`.
- **Interactive Accent Button**: Emerald Teal (`#00897b`) fill, white text, hovering to `#00796b`.
- **Input Fields**: Crisp white interior, 1px `#cbd5e1` border, `0.5rem` radius, focusing to a 1.5px `#00897b` ring.
