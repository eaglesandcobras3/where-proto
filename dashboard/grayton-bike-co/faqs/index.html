FAQs & knowledge

Answers visitors see on your listing and in search. Keep them short, factual, and up to date.

Published answers

  • Is there outdoor seating?

    Yes — patio or courtyard seating is part of the local vibe here.

  • Any dietary or dairy-free options?

    Most corridor cafes and kitchens can guide dairy-free and gluten-aware picks — confirm at the counter.

  • Where should I park or bike?

    Use posted Grayton Beach lots, or roll up on the Timpoochee Trail when bike racks are available.

  • How close is the beach?

    Most 30A outposts sit a short walk or golf-cart hop from a public walkover — check the map pin for the nearest access.
