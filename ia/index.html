Rules

  1. /businesses is a directory hub. Category groups only. No search box, no filters.
  2. /search is the only search page.
  3. Town pages own town-scoped category browsing via /town/{town}/{category}.
  4. Listings live on leaf pages only. /businesses, /towns, and /town/{town} never render business cards.
  5. Filtered or searched views are noindex, follow, canonical to the unfiltered URL.
  6. /ask is the community route. Threads at /ask/{question}.
