Route scaffold

No /businesses hub. /search is the finder. Town × category pages are the indexable leaves.

  1. There is no /businesses hub or /businesses/{category} leaf.
  2. /search is the business finder and stays noindex, follow.
  3. Town hubs are discovery surfaces with local businesses, plus /town/{town}/{leaf} leaves.
  4. /ask is the community route. Threads at /ask/{question}.
  5. Updates appear on town and business pages. /updates is footer only.
  6. Areas extend a town: shopping areas and neighborhoods.
