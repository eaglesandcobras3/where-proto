Route scaffold

No /businesses. /search is the finder. Town × category pages are the indexable leaves.

Rules

  1. There is no /businesses hub or /businesses/{category} leaf.
  2. /search is the business finder: town, category, tag, and open-now filters. Always noindex, follow. If the filters are exactly town + category, canonical points at that combo page.
  3. Town pages link to /town/{town}/{category}.
  4. Listings on indexable pages live only on /town/{town}/{category}. /towns and /town/{town} do not render business cards.
  5. /ask is the community route. Threads at /ask/{question}.
